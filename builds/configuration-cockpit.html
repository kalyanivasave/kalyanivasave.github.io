Configuration Portfolio Cockpit

All 21 current Lancer Worldwide beverage dispensers in one product line review: decide which configurations to push, reprice, restrict or retire, then check the rules that build them.

Start at Diagnose to see where the complexity sits, move the thresholds in Decide, and log the result for the review board. The Validate rules step uses real part numbers from Lancer's public manuals. Background: SKU rationalization, zombie variants, constraints, 150% BOM.

Portfolio volumes, prices, costs and margins are practice data, not Lancer data; replace them with your own on the Data step. The Validate rules step uses real part numbers and install rules from Lancer's public operation manuals for the IBD Series 4500 (PN 28-3194, November 2025), the CED 400 (PN 28-3141, September 2023) and the ICDI drop-ins, read from lancerworldwide.com. Its prices, lead times and the export voltage rule are practice values. Independent study, not affiliated with or endorsed by Lancer Worldwide.
