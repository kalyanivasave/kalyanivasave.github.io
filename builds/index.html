Labs

Working models I built to test what I think I know. The cockpit reviews a real manufacturer's whole product line. The Rules Lab looks at the rules behind a single configurable product, and the Portfolio Lab at the keep, retire and reprice decisions behind a high-variant one.

Portfolio and configuration

Configuration Portfolio Cockpit

Every current Lancer Worldwide beverage dispenser in one review: which configurations to push, reprice, restrict or retire, and whether the rules that build them still hold.

  • 41 configurations across all 21 current products, ranked A, B and C by revenue
  • Seven possible decisions, from quick-ship to retire, each with its reasons and the checks to make first
  • Configurators for three product families built from real part numbers in Lancer's public manuals, with regression tests
  • A decision log with announce, last order and last ship dates for the review board

Configuration lab

Configure-to-Order Rules Lab

A commercial mortise lock modelled the way configure-to-order manufacturers really do it: the rule set, a 150% BOM with conditional lines, routing and price build-up, plus a regression suite.

  • 8 constraints that guide the user instead of failing at order entry
  • A revision cut-in and a finish phase-out, driven by order date
  • 18 test configurations that run before every rule change

Variant Portfolio Lab

A task chair with 1,166,400 possible combinations, the same order of complexity as the Aeron. Decide which options to keep, retire or reprice, and see what each choice does to the business.

  • The full variant space and its long tail, counted live
  • Keep, retire and reprice decisions with their reasoning
  • Net contribution, cost to keep and valid configurations, before and after
