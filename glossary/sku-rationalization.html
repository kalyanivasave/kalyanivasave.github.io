SKU rationalization

Also called: Portfolio pruning, SKU reduction, variant reduction

A periodic review that removes products or options whose volume, margin or strategic value no longer justifies the cost of keeping them.

RelatedZombie variantsVariant managementProduct line managementEngineering change (ECR, ECO, ECN)

SKU rationalization is spring cleaning for a product portfolio. It is almost always overdue, almost always unpopular and almost always worth it.

How it works

  1. Get the real numbers. Volume and margin per SKU or option over two or three years, not one, so seasonal products aren't punished.
  2. Estimate the cost to keep. This is the part most reviews skip: price-book maintenance, configuration rules, test cases, stock, dealer content and change effort.
  3. Sort into keep, fix and retire. Some low-volume items are strategic (they win the big project). Some just need a price change. The rest go.
  4. Retire properly. Retiring is an engineering change: lifecycle state, Effectivity, stock disposition, service parts and a notice to dealers.

The trap: “but one customer orders it”

Every low-volume option has a defender, usually with a story about a large account. Sometimes the story is true. The test is simple: would that customer leave over this option, or would they accept the nearest alternative? Most accept the alternative, often without noticing.

Signals it's time

  • The price book grows every year and never shrinks.
  • Change impact analyses keep listing variants nobody has ordered in years.
  • Dealers can't explain the difference between two options.
  • A large share of options sell less than a handful of units a year.

Questions people ask

How often should a portfolio be rationalized?

Light reviews yearly, deeper reviews every few years, and a quick check whenever a new product launches into the same space as an old one.

Does removing options hurt sales?

Rarely, when the analysis is honest. Fewer, clearer choices often make products easier to buy. The risk is in removing an option that quietly wins large projects, which is why volume alone is not enough.
